The Dignity of a People

Who holds power, who can question it, and whose dignity is protected?

A nation’s strength is measured not by the grandeur of its throne, but by the dignity of its people.

Who holds power—and who can question it? What happens when a citizen challenges an injustice, a journalist investigates, or someone needs healthcare, education and opportunity?

Between Sunrise & Sunset will explore Japan and Morocco through politics, justice, human rights, economic development and everyday life—looking beyond appearances to examine how power is exercised and whom it serves.

Two nations. Two paths. Fifteen weekly episodes. The storm is coming.
